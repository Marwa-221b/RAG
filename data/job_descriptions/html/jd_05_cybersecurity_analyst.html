Cybersecurity Analyst (SOC L2/L3)

SecureNet Global  |  Posted: March 2025

📍 Location: Dubai, UAE / Hybrid 💼 Type: Full-Time, Permanent 💰 Salary: AED 18,000 – AED 26,000 / month 🗓️ Start Date: ASAP

About SecureNet Global

SecureNet Global is a leading managed security services provider (MSSP) operating across the GCC and MENA region. We protect 300+ enterprise clients including banks, telecoms, and government entities. Our 24x7 SOC in Dubai handles millions of security events daily using best-in-class SIEM and threat intelligence platforms. We are growing our team and looking for a talented Cybersecurity Analyst to join our SOC team.

WHAT YOU WILL DO — Key Responsibilities
  • Monitor, triage, and investigate security alerts from SIEM platforms (Splunk, Microsoft Sentinel, QRadar)
  • Perform Level 2/3 incident analysis and response — escalate critical incidents per IR playbook
  • Conduct threat hunting using MITRE ATT&CK framework
  • Analyse malware samples and perform basic reverse engineering (static/dynamic analysis)
  • Write detailed incident reports and RCAs for client stakeholders
  • Develop and tune SIEM correlation rules to reduce false positives
  • Collaborate with Tier 1 analysts — mentor and review their work
  • Participate in purple team exercises (Red Team / Blue Team)

Requirements

  1. Minimum 3 years experience in SOC / incident response / threat analysis role
  2. Hands-on experience with at least one SIEM platform (Splunk strongly preferred)
  3. Solid understanding of networking protocols: TCP/IP, DNS, HTTP/S, SMTP
  4. Familiarity with EDR solutions (CrowdStrike, SentinelOne, or similar)
  5. Knowledge of MITRE ATT&CK, Cyber Kill Chain frameworks
  6. Scripting ability: Python or PowerShell for automation
  7. At least ONE professional certification: CEH, GCIH, CompTIA Security+, CISSP (or in progress)
  8. Strong written and verbal communication — English required; Arabic a strong plus

Nice to Have / Preferred

  • Experience with SOAR platforms (Palo Alto XSOAR, Splunk SOAR)
  • Cloud security experience (Azure Sentinel, AWS GuardDuty)
  • Malware analysis / digital forensics background
  • Arabic language skills (reading incident documentation, communicating with Arab clients)
  • Experience in GCC banking or telecom sector

Tech Stack & Tools

Splunk Microsoft Sentinel QRadar CrowdStrike Palo Alto XSOAR Wireshark Nessus MITRE ATT&CK Python PowerShell Azure Defender Threat Intelligence Platforms
